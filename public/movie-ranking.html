My Top 5 Movies and Anime of All Time

Here are my personal top 5 movies and anime of all time, ranked according to my taste.

  1. Fight Club

    It follows an unnamed protagonist (Norton) who is discontented with his white-collar job. He forms a "fight club" with soap salesman Tyler Durden (Pitt), becoming embroiled in a violent and anarchistic world.

    Learn more about Fight Club

  2. Inception

    A thief who steals corporate secrets through dream-sharing technology is given the inverse task of planting an idea into the mind of a C.E.O., but his tragic past may doom the project and his team to disaster.

    Learn more about Inception

  3. Bleach

    Bleach is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Tite Kubo. It follows Ichigo Kurosaki, a teenager who gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and must protect both the living and the dead from evil spirits.

    Learn more about Bleach

  4. The Wrong Way to Use Healing Magic

    The Wrong Way to Use Healing Magic is a Japanese light novel series written by Kurokata and illustrated by KeG. It follows a young man who discovers that he has the ability to heal others, but his unconventional methods lead to unexpected adventures.

    Learn more about The Wrong Way to Use Healing Magic

  5. That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime

    That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime is a Japanese light novel series written by Fuse and illustrated by Mitz Vah. It follows a man who is reincarnated as a slime in a fantasy world and begins building a new life filled with adventure.
